Privacy Policy for Account-a-Bill

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Account-a-Bill (“the App”) is a personal finance tracking application that runs entirely on your device.

1. Data Collection

Account-a-Bill does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or financial data on external servers.

All information you enter (goals, income, expenses, mileage, time logs, tax settings, etc.) is stored only on your device using the browser’s local storage and IndexedDB.

2. Location Data

The App may request access to your device’s location only when you choose to use the GPS auto-mileage feature.

  • Location data is used solely to calculate the distance of a business trip.
  • Location data is processed on your device in real time.
  • Location data is never uploaded, shared, or stored on any server.
  • You can deny location permission; the rest of the App continues to work normally.
  • You can stop a GPS trip at any time.

3. Analytics & Tracking

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking scripts.

4. Backups

Any backup files you create (JSON exports) are generated and saved entirely under your control. The App never uploads these files.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the GitHub repository:
https://github.com/synchancybersecurity/account-a-bill

This policy applies to both the web version and any native wrappers (Android / iOS) of Account-a-Bill.
